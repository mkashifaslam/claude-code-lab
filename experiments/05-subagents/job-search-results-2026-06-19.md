## Key Insights & Red Flags

### Green Flags (Apply Immediately)
✅ Explicitly states "Remote" or "Work from anywhere"  
✅ Tech stack lists MERN or 4+ core skills (MongoDB, Express, React, Node, JS, TS, HTML, CSS)  
✅ "Willing to mentor junior developer" or "Entry-level friendly" in description  
✅ Posted within last 14 days  
✅ Salary stated in USD/EUR (international pay)  
✅ Pakistan applicants explicitly welcomed or "Worldwide" location  
✅ Application process is straightforward (direct apply, no gatekeeping tests)

### Red Flags (Deprioritize)
🚩 "US/EU only" or "Must be authorized to work in [country]" without mentioning Pakistan  
🚩 "On-site required" but labeled as remote  
🚩 3+ years minimum experience despite "junior" title  
🚩 Vague tech stack (just "web developer") with no MERN mention  
🚩 No salary range or extremely low range (e.g., <$500/month for 40 hrs/week)  
🚩 Complex multi-round screening before first technical interview  
🚩 No application link or outdated posting (>60 days old)
